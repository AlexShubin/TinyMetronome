# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 6 October 2026

Tiny Metronome ("the app") runs entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no network communication. It does not contact any server operated by us or any third party.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores nothing. Tempo, click sound and beat settings live in memory while the app is open and are reset when it quits.

## Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it complies with COPPA and similar regulations by default.
